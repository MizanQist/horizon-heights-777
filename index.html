The building

Plot 777 · CAD Zone A07 · Wuse II

Eighteen residences, three to a floor.

Heights 777 is a seven-storey residential building on Parakou Street, in the residential heart of Wuse II. Eighteen residences occupy six floors above an open arrival and parking level, served by two lifts and three stairs: a lift and main stair at each end of the lobby, and a back stair by the courtyard.

Every residence opens onto a planted balcony behind a frameless glass balustrade, and every slab edge carries a continuous strip of warm light. Your residence is the centre one of the three on the fifth floor, between the two lift cores.

A quiet street between Maitama and the city.

Parakou Street runs through the residential heart of Wuse II, a few minutes from the restaurants and shops of Aminu Kano Crescent, with Maitama to the north and the Central Business District to the south-east. Some maps call it Parakou Crescent.

Your residence

Apartment 14 · fifth floor · centre

The centre residence, five floors up.

Apartment 14 sits at the middle of the fifth floor, between the two lift cores, with the west penthouse on one side and the east residence on the other. The living room faces the street; the kitchen and both bedrooms run along the quiet north side, and there is a balcony front and rear.
